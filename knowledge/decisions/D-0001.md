# D-0001 — The cab repair is a scripted edit of the bundle, not a re-export and not a runtime fix

**Decision**: The generator's OBJ and MTL are committed unchanged and never edited by
hand. `devtools/art/build.py` reads them, identifies pieces by material and bounds,
applies the repairs -- the solid cab replaced by floor, walls and bulkhead; the roof,
pillars, glass and mirrors raised 8 px; the seats raised; the gauge cluster flipped and
moved to the dash with new needles; the steering wheel raised and brought forward -- and
writes the meshes, the atlas and the profile, whose seat points and pivots come from the
same constants. The protocol never compensates for the bundle.

**Why**: Measured on the bundle, the cab could not seat a player (the eye 4 px above the
roof, inside a solid block) and the dials faced the nose from inside the hood; something
had to move. A re-export from the generator would have been a new bundle to measure
again; a hand edit of a thousand-vertex OBJ is not reviewable and not repeatable; a
runtime fix would put one vehicle's mistakes into the protocol every vehicle shares.
A script over the box list is reviewable line by line, reproducible from the repository,
and keeps the profile's numbers and the mesh's in one place.

**Alternatives rejected**: above.

**Links**: `devtools/art/build.py`, `src/gametest/.../TrailblazerBooth` (the dash photo
is the check that fails on the unrepaired bundle).
